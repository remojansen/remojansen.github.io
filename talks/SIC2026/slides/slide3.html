AI Flips the Equation

Generative models can synthesize massive blocks of software near-instantaneously. However, this flips our developmental bottlenecks upside down.

Traditional Era (Before AI)

Writing Code: Expensive, slow, constrained by physical key inputs

Verification: Manageable; author reviews localized logic mappings

AI-Assisted Era (Now)

Writing Code: Virtually free, instantaneous

Verification: Extremely expensive, cognitively taxing

The core engineering roadblock has shifted from generating output to verifying correctness. Optimizing for speed of writing no longer makes sense — we now need to optimize for speed of verification.
