Beyond Code Generation

Intelligence without constraints is unpredictable.
Constraints turn intelligence into reliable systems.

Without Constraints

  • AI may skip approvals
  • Steps executed out of order
  • Inconsistent decisions
  • Difficult to audit or explain

With Constraints (e.g. Finite State Machines - FSMs)

  • Every transition is valid
  • Business rules are enforced
  • Invalid actions are impossible
  • Predictable, auditable automation
